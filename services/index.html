AI Development

Big Futur Digital develops AI chatbots and retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) systems that help customers and employees find relevant information and complete useful tasks.

We build around your approved content, documents, and processes. Each project defines the data sources, user access, integrations, and expected behaviour before development.

What We Can Deliver

  • Website chatbots and AI assistants
  • Customer enquiry and support chatbots
  • Internal company knowledge assistants
  • Document search and question-answering systems
  • RAG integration with approved knowledge sources
  • Business system and API integrations
  • Response testing and ongoing improvements

What Is a RAG System?

A RAG system retrieves relevant information from your documents or knowledge base before generating an answer. This helps an AI assistant respond using your business content. Source references, access controls, testing, and human handover are defined in the scope.
